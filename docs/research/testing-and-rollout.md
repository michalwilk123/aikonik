# Testing and rollout: starter scope

The challenge brief (`hubmi.pdf`, sections IV and VII) defines the tester as participation, evaluation, feedback and improvement support. Middleman Innowacji adapts an innovation to an institution's service needs. The prototype prepares drafts in conversation; it does not register participants or launch services.

## Methodology

- [Nesta: Prototype testing plan](https://www.nesta.org.uk/toolkit/prototype-testing-plan/) was read directly. It recommends a hypothesis, a quick prototype and repeated feedback at different stages. The testing agent uses those steps to draft a small test rather than invent outcomes.
- [ROPS: Inkubator Włączenia Społecznego 2.0](https://rops.krakow.pl/realizowane-projekty-i-zadania/inkubator-wlaczenia-spolecznego-20,o-projekcie) describes tests of products, tools, services and support models for people experiencing exclusion. ## Agent boundaries

`agents/testuj-innowacje` owns its test plan and feedback panel (amber). `agents/wdrazanie-innowacji` owns its institutional service brief (rose). Both export their own prompt, knowledge and components; neither imports another agent's UI. Proposed targets, costs and dates must be distinguished from user data and documented evidence.
